# Author-Approved Evidence Baseline

This repository is for a standalone agent skill that helps ordinary ChatGPT and AI users find legitimate deals and coupons with immediate consumer value and broad appeal.

The project remains planning-only until the author approves `VISION.md` through the required interactive review.

No product or skill implementation is authorized by this baseline.

The planning work must account for users with web-enabled AI and users whose AI cannot browse.

The project must not fabricate discounts, coupon validity, savings, urgency, seller safety, or price history.

The planning work must address stale coupons, total purchase cost, seller legitimacy, affiliate conflicts, privacy, regional limits, and accessibility.

Claims about deals and savings require attributable evidence with clear limits and freshness.

The smallest useful first version must be defined before implementation is considered.

This baseline records author-approved intent and constraints only; proposed principles and product choices remain unapproved until the author reviews them.
